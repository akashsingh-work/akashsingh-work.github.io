Project header Customer Support Knowledge Base DEMO PROJECT A structured SaaS knowledge base covering customer FAQs, troubleshooting procedures, support documentation, and escalation workflows. Tags: Customer Support · Knowledge Base · Troubleshooting 01 — Project Overview Fictional business: FlowDesk Industry: SaaS / Business Productivity This project demonstrates the design of a centralized customer-support knowledge base for a fictional SaaS platform. The system is designed to help customers find answers quickly while giving support agents consistent troubleshooting procedures and escalation guidelines. Objective - Standardize responses to common customer questions - Create self-service support resources - Document troubleshooting procedures - Define clear escalation conditions - Improve consistency across support interactions 02 — Knowledge Base Structure Create five cards: Getting Started Account creation, projects, team invitations and basic product guidance. Account & Billing Password resets, account information, payments and subscription requests. Product Support Tasks, notifications, file uploads and common product issues. Troubleshooting Step-by-step diagnostic procedures and information-gathering checklists. Support & Escalation Ticket documentation, priority levels and escalation procedures. 03 — Sample Support Articles Create 5 expandable cards. Password Reset Customer cannot access their account because they have forgotten their password. Resolution: Use the Forgot Password process, verify the reset email, check spam/junk folders and confirm successful login. Escalate when: The reset process repeatedly fails or the customer cannot access their registered email. Payment Issue Customer reports that a subscription payment failed. Resolution: Verify the account, check the payment status, confirm the payment method and guide the customer through an appropriate retry. Escalate when: Repeated valid payment attempts fail or the issue appears to involve the payment system. Tasks Not Syncing Recently created or updated tasks aren't appearing correctly. Resolution: Check connectivity, refresh the workspace, confirm the task was saved, sign in again and test another supported environment. Escalate when: Multiple tasks remain unsynchronized after standard troubleshooting. Notifications Not Working Customer isn't receiving expected product notifications. Resolution: Check FlowDesk notification settings, browser/device permissions, account configuration and whether other notifications work. Escalate when: Notifications remain unavailable after standard troubleshooting. File Upload Problem Customer is unable to upload a supported file. Resolution: Check file type/size, connection, try another file, refresh the project and test another supported browser. Escalate when: Multiple supported files fail or the issue appears to affect multiple users. 04 — Troubleshooting Framework Use the workflow visually: Customer Contact ↓ Identify Issue ↓ Verify Account ↓ Categorize Request ↓ Search Knowledge Base ↓ Apply Standard Troubleshooting ↓ Confirm Resolution ↓ Resolved? → Yes: Document & Close → No: Escalate With Complete Notes 05 — Escalation Matrix Priority Example Action Low General product question Provide information / KB article Medium Individual feature issue Troubleshoot and document High Account access problem Immediate troubleshooting + escalation if unresolved Critical Suspected widespread issue Collect impact details + technical escalation Escalation notes should include: - Issue summary - Customer/account information - Error message - Troubleshooting already performed - Device/browser - Screenshots where relevant - Customer impact - Time of occurrence 06 — Support Documentation Standard Every support interaction should contain: Issue — What happened? Customer Impact — What can the customer not do? Troubleshooting Performed — What has already been attempted? Result — Was the issue resolved? Next Action — Close, follow up or escalate. Customer Communication — What was communicated to the customer? 07 — Tools & Skills Tools Notion · Google Docs · Excel / Google Sheets Skills demonstrated Customer Support · Troubleshooting · Documentation · Knowledge Management · Ticket Handling · Escalation Management · Process Organization 08 — Outcome Created a structured SaaS support knowledge base covering common customer questions, troubleshooting procedures, internal support guidance, ticket documentation and escalation criteria. Key takeaway: The project demonstrates an organized approach to turning recurring customer issues into reusable support documentation and repeatable troubleshooting processes.
