# Sales Pipeline Management

## Overview

A structured sales pipeline helps businesses track potential customers from initial contact until conversion.

This document demonstrates a process for managing leads, tracking opportunities, maintaining follow-ups, and improving conversion rates through CRM workflows.

---

# Sales Pipeline Stages

## Stage 1: New Lead

### Description:

A potential customer has entered the business pipeline through a marketing channel or direct inquiry.

### Lead Sources:

- Website forms
- LinkedIn
- Email inquiries
- Referrals
- Social media campaigns

### Required Actions:

- Create CRM record
- Capture customer information
- Identify initial requirements
- Assign follow-up task

---

# Stage 2: Initial Contact

### Description:

First communication has been completed with the prospect.

### Actions:

- Understand customer requirements
- Identify business needs
- Explain available solutions
- Record conversation notes

### CRM Updates:

- Contact date
- Communication method
- Customer requirements
- Next follow-up date

---

# Stage 3: Qualified Lead

### Description:

The prospect has a genuine requirement and matches the business criteria.

Qualification Factors:

- Business requirement identified
- Budget discussed
- Decision-maker identified
- Timeline confirmed

### Actions:

- Schedule detailed discussion
- Prepare suitable solution
- Move opportunity forward

---

# Stage 4: Proposal / Solution Presented

### Description:

The customer has received a detailed solution, pricing, or service information.

Actions:

- Share proposal
- Answer customer questions
- Handle objections
- Track customer feedback

CRM Updates:

- Proposal date
- Customer response
- Expected decision date

---

# Stage 5: Negotiation

### Description:

Customer is interested but requires further discussion before final decision.

Activities:

- Discuss requirements
- Address concerns
- Modify solution if required
- Maintain regular follow-ups

---

# Stage 6: Closed Won

### Description:

Customer has accepted the solution.

Actions:

- Update CRM status
- Begin onboarding process
- Maintain customer records
- Schedule future follow-ups

---

# Stage 7: Closed Lost

### Description:

Opportunity was not converted.

Possible Reasons:

- Budget limitations
- Customer selected another provider
- Requirement changed
- Timing issue

Actions:

- Record reason
- Analyze improvement areas
- Keep relationship open for future opportunities

---

# Follow-up Management

Effective follow-ups include:

## Scheduled Tasks

Examples:

- Call customer after proposal
- Send additional information
- Check customer feedback

## Communication Tracking

Record:

- Date
- Communication method
- Discussion summary
- Next action

---

# Pipeline Benefits

A structured pipeline helps businesses:

- Avoid missed opportunities
- Improve sales visibility
- Increase conversion rates
- Maintain organized customer communication
- Forecast future business growth
