# CRM Management System

## Overview

A simulated CRM management system designed for a growing business to organize customer information, track sales opportunities, manage follow-ups, and improve customer relationship management.

This project demonstrates my understanding of CRM operations, customer data organization, sales pipeline management, and reporting processes.

---

## Business Scenario

A growing company was managing customer information through multiple spreadsheets and manual processes.

The business needed a structured CRM system to:

- Maintain organized customer records
- Track customer interactions
- Manage leads and opportunities
- Improve follow-up processes
- Monitor business performance

---

## Solution Provided

Created a CRM framework including:

- Customer database structure
- Lead management process
- Sales pipeline stages
- Follow-up tracking system
- Reporting dashboard structure

---

# CRM Components

## 1. Customer Database Management

Created a structured customer information system containing:

- Customer details
- Company information
- Contact history
- Customer status
- Follow-up requirements

---

## 2. Lead Management

Designed a process to track leads from initial contact to conversion.

Stages include:

- New Lead
- Contacted
- Qualified
- Proposal Sent
- Negotiation
- Closed Won/Lost

---

## 3. Customer Follow-up System

Created a follow-up process to ensure:

- No customer is missed
- Tasks are completed on time
- Communication history is maintained

---

## 4. Reporting Structure

Tracked important business metrics:

- New leads
- Active customers
- Conversion rate
- Pending follow-ups
- Pipeline value
