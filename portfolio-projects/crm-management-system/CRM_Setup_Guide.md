# CRM Setup Guide

## Overview

This guide explains the structure and setup process for a customer relationship management system.

The objective is to create an organized system where businesses can manage customer information, track interactions, monitor sales opportunities, and improve customer relationships.

---

# 1. CRM Structure

A CRM system should contain the following sections:

## Contacts

Stores individual customer information.

Required fields:

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Company Name
- Customer Type
- Lead Source
- Status
- Last Interaction Date
- Notes

Purpose:

Maintains complete customer history and enables personalized communication.

---

## Companies

Stores business-level information.

Required fields:

- Company Name
- Industry
- Company Size
- Contact Person
- Business Requirements
- Account Status

Purpose:

Helps manage relationships with organizations and business accounts.

---

## Deals / Opportunities

Tracks potential revenue opportunities.

Required information:

- Deal Name
- Customer
- Deal Value
- Sales Stage
- Expected Closing Date
- Assigned Team Member

Purpose:

Helps sales teams monitor opportunities and conversions.

---

# 2. Pipeline Setup

A standard customer pipeline:

## Stage 1: New Lead

Customer or business has shown initial interest.

Action:

- Collect information
- Understand requirements
- Create CRM record

---

## Stage 2: Contacted

Initial communication has been completed.

Action:

- Discuss requirements
- Schedule follow-up
- Update CRM notes

---

## Stage 3: Qualified

Lead has potential and meets business criteria.

Action:

- Identify needs
- Provide relevant solution
- Move towards proposal

---

## Stage 4: Proposal Sent

Customer has received pricing or service details.

Action:

- Track response
- Handle queries
- Follow up

---

## Stage 5: Closed Won

Customer has accepted the offer.

Action:

- Update customer status
- Begin onboarding
- Maintain relationship

---

## Stage 6: Closed Lost

Opportunity was not converted.

Action:

- Record reason
- Analyze improvement areas
- Maintain future communication possibility

---

# 3. Task Management

CRM tasks should include:

- Follow-up reminders
- Customer calls
- Email responses
- Meetings
- Internal coordination

Benefits:

- Prevents missed activities
- Improves productivity
- Creates accountability

---

# 4. Data Management Guidelines

Maintain CRM accuracy by:

- Updating customer information regularly
- Removing duplicate records
- Adding interaction notes
- Updating customer status
- Recording important conversations

---

# 5. Customer Interaction Tracking

Every interaction should include:

## Date:

When communication happened

## Channel:

- Email
- Phone
- Chat
- Meeting

## Summary:

Brief description of discussion

## Next Action:

Required follow-up or resolution

---

# 6. CRM Best Practices

## Maintain Data Quality

Accurate data helps teams make better decisions.

## Follow Standard Processes

Consistent workflows improve customer experience.

## Update Records Immediately

Delayed updates can create communication gaps.

## Use Reports Regularly

Reports help identify:

- Performance issues
- Sales opportunities
- Customer trends
