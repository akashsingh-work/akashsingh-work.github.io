# Customer Support Operations Dashboard

## Overview

A simulated customer support operations system designed for a growing SaaS company to improve customer communication, ticket tracking, escalation handling, and reporting.

This project demonstrates my ability to organize customer support workflows, maintain customer records, create support documentation, and monitor key performance indicators.

---

## Business Scenario

A SaaS company was receiving increasing customer queries through multiple channels but lacked a structured support process.

The objective was to create a simple support framework that helps the team:

- Track customer issues
- Prioritize urgent cases
- Maintain customer records
- Reduce unresolved tickets
- Improve response consistency

---

## Solution Provided

Created a customer support workflow including:

- Ticket tracking system
- Issue categorization process
- Customer escalation workflow
- Support SOP documentation
- Email response templates
- Performance reporting structure

---

## Deliverables

### 1. Support Ticket Tracker

A structured system to track:

- Customer information
- Issue category
- Priority level
- Status
- Assigned team member
- Resolution details

### 2. Support SOP Documentation

Created step-by-step procedures for:

- Customer verification
- Issue identification
- Troubleshooting
- Escalation handling
- Resolution communication

### 3. Customer Communication Templates

Prepared professional templates for:

- Customer complaints
- Follow-ups
- Resolutions
- Escalations

### 4. KPI Reporting

Tracked important support metrics:

- Total tickets received
- Resolution rate
- Response time
- Escalation rate
- Customer satisfaction
