# Customer Support Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## Purpose

This document defines the standard process for handling customer queries, troubleshooting issues, escalating cases, and ensuring consistent customer support quality.

The objective is to provide customers with accurate, timely, and professional assistance while maintaining proper documentation.

---

# 1. Customer Query Handling Process

## Step 1: Customer Identification

Before assisting the customer:

- Verify customer details
- Confirm account information
- Understand the customer's reason for contacting support
- Review previous interactions if available

---

## Step 2: Understand Customer Concern

Identify:

- Problem category
- Customer expectations
- Urgency level
- Business impact

Common categories:

- Account issues
- Billing/payment queries
- Technical problems
- Product/service questions
- Feature requests
- Complaints

---

# 2. Issue Categorization

## Priority Levels

### High Priority

Examples:

- Service outage
- Payment failure affecting customer access
- Security-related concerns
- Multiple users affected

Expected action:

- Immediate investigation
- Escalate if required
- Provide regular updates

---

### Medium Priority

Examples:

- Feature-related questions
- Account updates
- General troubleshooting

Expected action:

- Resolve within standard response timeline
- Document solution

---

### Low Priority

Examples:

- General information requests
- Product questions
- Feedback

Expected action:

- Provide accurate information
- Close after resolution

---

# 3. Troubleshooting Workflow

## Basic Troubleshooting Steps

1. Understand the issue completely

2. Collect required information

3. Check available documentation/knowledge base

4. Perform basic troubleshooting steps

5. Confirm whether the issue is resolved

6. Document the final solution

---

# 4. Escalation Process

Cases should be escalated when:

- Issue requires technical team assistance
- Customer problem cannot be resolved through available steps
- Multiple customers are experiencing the same problem
- The issue impacts business operations

## Escalation Information

Every escalation should include:

- Customer details
- Issue summary
- Troubleshooting performed
- Screenshots/details if available
- Expected resolution

---

# 5. Customer Communication Guidelines

## Communication Principles

Support communication should be:

- Clear
- Professional
- Empathetic
- Solution-focused

Avoid:

- Technical jargon without explanation
- Blaming customers
- Providing unclear timelines

---

# 6. Follow-up Process

After providing a solution:

- Confirm customer satisfaction
- Check if additional assistance is required
- Update ticket status
- Document final resolution

---

# 7. CRM Documentation Guidelines

Every customer interaction should contain:

- Customer information
- Issue category
- Conversation summary
- Action taken
- Current status
- Next steps

Proper documentation helps teams:

- Reduce repeated work
- Understand customer history
- Improve service quality

---

# 8. Quality Checklist

Before closing a ticket:

☐ Customer concern understood  
☐ Correct solution provided  
☐ Customer informed about resolution  
☐ CRM updated  
☐ Ticket status updated  
☐ Follow-up completed if required
