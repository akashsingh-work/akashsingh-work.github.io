# Customer Support Metrics


## First Response Time (FRT)

Measures how quickly a support team responds after receiving a customer request.


Formula:

Total response time / Number of tickets


Importance:

Helps measure support responsiveness.



## Average Resolution Time (ART)

Measures the average time required to solve customer issues.


Formula:

Total resolution time / Resolved tickets


Importance:

Shows operational efficiency.



## First Contact Resolution (FCR)

Measures issues solved during the first interaction.


Formula:

Resolved on first contact / Total tickets × 100


Importance:

Higher FCR improves customer satisfaction.



## Ticket Volume

Tracks the number of incoming support requests over time.


Importance:

Helps identify workload patterns.
