# E-commerce Customer Experience System

## Overview

A simulated customer experience framework designed for an e-commerce business to improve customer support, order management, complaint handling, and customer retention.

---

# Business Scenario

An online store receives large numbers of customer requests related to:

- Orders
- Delivery
- Payments
- Returns
- Refunds
- Product information

The goal is to create a structured support system.

---

# Solution Provided

Created:

- Order support workflow
- Refund management process
- Customer FAQ system
- Chat response templates
- Retention strategy
