# Customer Retention Strategy

## Overview

A framework designed to improve customer satisfaction and encourage repeat business.

---

# Strategies

## 1. Follow-up Communication

Contact customers after resolution to confirm satisfaction.

---

## 2. Personalized Support

Use customer history to provide better assistance.

---

## 3. Feedback Collection

Collect customer opinions to improve services.

---

## 4. Loyalty Building

Encourage repeat customers through:

- Better communication
- Faster support
- Personalized experiences

---

# Expected Results

- Higher customer satisfaction
- Increased repeat purchases
- Reduced complaints
- Better brand reputation
