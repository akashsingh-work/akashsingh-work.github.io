# Customer Support Process


## Ticket Lifecycle


Customer Issue Received

↓

Issue Categorization

↓

Troubleshooting

↓

Resolution Provided

↓

Customer Confirmation

↓

Ticket Closure



## Escalation Process


Level 1 Support:

Basic troubleshooting and account assistance


Level 2 Support:

Technical investigation and advanced issues


Level 3 Support:

Engineering/product team escalation
