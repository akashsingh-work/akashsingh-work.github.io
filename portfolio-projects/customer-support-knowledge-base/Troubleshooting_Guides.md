# Troubleshooting Guides


## Login Issues

### Problem

Customer unable to access account.


### Troubleshooting Steps

1. Verify customer identity
2. Confirm registered email/account details
3. Check account status
4. Reset password if required
5. Escalate if issue persists


### Resolution

Customer access restored after verification.


---


## Billing Issues

### Problem

Customer reports incorrect billing information.


### Troubleshooting Steps

1. Verify billing details
2. Check payment history
3. Confirm recent transactions
4. Update information if authorized


### Resolution

Billing information corrected.
